# Terms and privacy

## Terms of use, in short

* List only properties you own or are authorised to sell or let, with honest photos and details.
* Pay and agree deals through Sheda Solutions, so escrow protects both sides.
* Treat everyone with respect, without discrimination.
* Keep your account and wallet secure. Blockchain transactions can't be reversed.
* Sheda Solutions does not guarantee any return, rent or resale price on Sheda Collections.

## Your profile and privacy

* Your name, photo, username, location, agency and rating are on your profile for other users.
* Your email and phone number are shown only to someone you have an appointment with, so you can reach each other.
* Details you add, like your work and languages, are shown to the people you choose.
* Your payment details, appointments and appointment hours are never shown on your profile.

Questions? Email [support@sheda.app](mailto:support@sheda.app).
