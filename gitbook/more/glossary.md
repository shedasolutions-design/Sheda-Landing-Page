# Glossary

**Escrow**: a smart contract that holds a payment until the deal is done. Nobody can spend it early.

**Smart contract**: a program on the blockchain that follows fixed, public rules and can't be quietly changed.

**Blockchain**: a shared public record that anyone can check. Sheda Solutions uses NEAR.

**Wallet**: your account on the blockchain. On Sheda Solutions it's built in and kept on your device.

**Recovery phrase**: 12 words that restore your wallet on any device.

**Refundable damage deposit**: money a renter pays with the rent, held in escrow during the lease against damage, and returned if there's none.

**Appointment**: a time the buyer asks for to inspect a property, approved by the seller.

**Sheda Collections**: fractional ownership on Sheda Solutions; owning shares in a specific flat.

**Raise**: a round in which a builder collects money from many investors to build or buy.

**Project pack**: the builder's documents for a raise: title, approvals, costs, timeline and agreements.

**Phase**: a stage of building. The builder is paid one phase at a time, after inspection.

**Objection window**: the 7 days owners have to object before a phase is paid.

**Vault**: a contract for each flat that receives its rent and pays its owners.

**Stake**: a builder's own money held as cover for investors in their raises.

**Pledge**: a manager's own written terms that investors can hold them to.

**Share market**: where owners sell and buy shares between themselves.

**Council**: the account that settles disputes and controls contract changes; it needs several members to sign.

**Testnet / mainnet**: the NEAR test network (test money) and the main network (real money).
