# FAQ

<details>

<summary>Who runs the deals on Sheda Solutions?</summary>

You do. Sheda Solutions is a decentralised market: builders, investors, property managers, landlords, buyers and renters each run their own part of a property's life. Sheda Solutions provides the rails, the escrow, the open records and the tools, and the money sits in smart contracts, not with Sheda Solutions.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How does escrow protect my money?</summary>

When you pay, the money doesn't go straight to the other side. It's held by a smart contract and released only when the deal is complete. Not a buyer, a seller or even Sheda Solutions can take it early.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is my property ownership secure?</summary>

Ownership is recorded on the NEAR blockchain, so nobody can quietly change it. You can check it yourself, any time, without asking anyone.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I need to understand crypto?</summary>

No. The wallet is built into the app and there are no network fees to pay. You browse, pay and sign like any other app.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What does it cost?</summary>

Browsing and listing are free, with no agent fees and no network fees. When a payment completes, a 2% fee is taken by the smart contract, in the open. A cancelled or refunded deal costs nothing. See [Fees](../guides/fees.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can several people ask for the same appointment time?</summary>

Yes. Nothing is booked until the seller approves it. The seller can approve any request, or decline with a note and a suggested time.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What if something goes wrong in a deal?</summary>

Either side can raise a dispute from the deal until the payment is released. The money stays in escrow while it's reviewed. See [Trust and safety](../litepaper/trust-and-safety.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is my return guaranteed if I invest?</summary>

No. Rent shown on a raise is the builder's estimate. Read [Risks of investing](../guides/investing.md#risks-of-investing) first.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I use Sheda Solutions today?</summary>

Yes, at [market.sheda.app](https://market.sheda.app). During early access everything runs on the NEAR test network, so you can explore every feature risk-free while the independent security audit is completed.

</details>
