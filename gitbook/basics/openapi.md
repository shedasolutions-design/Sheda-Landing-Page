# Dispute resolution

**How It Works**

The integration of smart contracts within property transactions revolutionizes how disputes and inspections are handled:

* Inspections Before Payment: Before finalizing a transaction, the buyer books an appointment to inspect the property in person, and funds are released only after the buyer accepts the agreement. In Sheda Collections, the property manager chosen by the investors inspects each building phase before the builder is paid, and investors have 7 days to object.
* Escrow Hold: From the moment an offer is accepted until the payment is released, either side can raise a dispute from the deal. The payment stays in escrow while it is reviewed.
* Council Ruling: Today, disputes are ruled on by the Sheda Solutions council, a multi-signature account that needs several of its members to sign, and the ruling is enforced by the contract itself.
* Automated Mediation: As we grow, AI agents will help resolve disputes more efficiently, with outcomes enforced by the contract itself.

Pre-set Mechanism: Within the smart contract, specific conditions or triggers for disputes are defined. For example, if there's a disagreement about property condition or payment terms not being met, or a landlord claims against a refundable damage deposit within 7 days of a lease ending, these conditions engage the dispute process.

Mediation or Arbitration: Once triggered, the process can either:

* * Mediate by suggesting neutral parties or an AI agent to help resolve the dispute. This could involve selecting from a pool of trusted mediators or arbitrators registered on the platform, whose services are invoked through the contract.
  * Arbitrate by having pre-agreed rules or an automated system that decides based on the data and terms within the contract. This might involve an AI agent analyzing the deal's record, documents and messages, or might escalate to a human arbitrator if the situation is complex. People always make the final call.
* Outcome Enforcement: The beauty of smart contracts is their self-executing nature. The outcome of mediation or arbitration is automatically enforced by the contract:
* * If mediation leads to an agreement, the contract can release funds, update ownership records, or take any action agreed upon by the parties.
  * If arbitration results in a decision, the smart contract will execute this decision, like refunding money, paying the seller, splitting a damage deposit, or even nullifying the deal if that's the resolution.

This process reduces the need for external legal proceedings, which can be time-consuming, costly, and less transparent. By embedding mediation and arbitration into the smart contract:

* Trust is Enhanced: Both parties know that a fair mechanism for resolving disputes exists, which is transparent and bound by the terms they agreed to.
* Efficiency Increases: Disputes can be resolved much quicker without the need for courts or lengthy negotiations.
* Cost is Reduced: Legal fees and court costs are minimized or eliminated.
* Transparency is Maintained: All actions and outcomes are recorded on the blockchain, providing an immutable record of the dispute resolution.

This system leverages blockchain's capabilities for not just executing contracts but also for managing and resolving conflicts in a manner that aligns with the decentralized ethos of our vision.
