# Executive summary

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<mark style="background-color:red;">**SHEDA SOLUTIONS**</mark> is set to redefine the landscape of real estate transactions by leveraging blockchain smart contracts to create a fully peer-to-peer (P2P), decentralised market putting control in your hands. Builders, investors, property managers, landlords, buyers and renters each run their own part of a property's life, while Sheda Solutions provides the rails: escrow, open records and the tools. This paper outlines our approach to making real estate operations more transparent, secure, accessible, and efficient. Our focus is providing homes where the demand is, starting with student housing and extending to urban markets, introducing:

Smart contract-based solutions for escrow, dispute resolution and automated transactions.

Decentralized marketplace for direct property transactions: Rent now, Buy now or Make an offer, with every payment held in escrow.

Fractional ownership, through Sheda Collections, for funding and managing real estate projects, with investors voting for the manager, approving each building phase and claiming their rent directly.

We will explore how AI can further enhance these solutions, providing intelligent automation, faster dispute resolution and analytics to support all stakeholders in the real estate ecosystem.
