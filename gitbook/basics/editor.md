# Executive summary

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<mark style="background-color:red;">**SHEDA SOLUTIONS**</mark> is a decentralised market for real estate operations, built on the NEAR blockchain. It replaces the agent, the cash deposit and the paper file with three things: **smart contract escrow**, **open on-chain records**, and **tools that let every participant run their own part of a property's life**.

## The problem

Property transactions in Nigeria and across Africa run on blind trust. Buyers and renters pay agents and deposits up front with no protection; the same home can be sold twice because ownership is hard to verify; agreements live on paper; and investing in property requires a large lump sum with no say over how the money is used. Demand for housing, from students relocating for school to workers moving into growing cities, far exceeds supply, and new homes are often built where they're least needed.

## What Sheda Solutions does

**1. A peer-to-peer marketplace.** Owners list homes and land; each listing is minted as a property record on the blockchain under its owner's account. Buyers and renters choose **Buy now**, **Rent now** or **Make an offer**, and their money is locked in escrow immediately. The seller accepts an offer, the buyer books an appointment to inspect, the seller sends the agreement, and only when the buyer accepts it is the payment released, after a 24-hour hold. Rentals add a refundable damage deposit held in escrow for the whole lease.

**2. Sheda Collections: fractional ownership with control.** Builders raise from many investors to build or buy. Each investor owns fungible shares in one specific flat. The money waits in the raise contract and is released phase by phase, only after the property manager the investors voted for inspects each phase and investors have had 7 days to object. When the building is let, each flat's rent flows into its own vault contract, which splits it among owners pro rata. Owners claim their rent directly and trade shares on an open share market. Builders post stake as cover for investors if a build stops.

**3. Infrastructure that hides the blockchain.** Every user gets a NEAR wallet at sign-up, kept on their own device and backed up to Google or a 12-word phrase. Network fees are sponsored, so nobody needs to hold crypto. Payments settle in dollar stablecoins (USDC and USDT), and prices are displayed in 24 currencies, including naira.

## Why it works

* **Trust is replaced by rules.** Neither party, nor Sheda Solutions, can take escrowed money outside the contract's rules.
* **Supply follows demand.** Every listing, deal and rent payment lands on one shared record, so the market shows where homes are wanted, and builders can raise there, starting with student housing near campuses and expanding to urban markets.
* **The fee is small and only earned on success.** 2% of a completed payment, taken by the contract in the open, and nothing on refunds.

## Where it stands

The marketplace and Sheda Collections are live in early access on the NEAR test network. Mainnet launch, starting in Lagos with Android and iOS apps, follows an independent security audit. Next on the roadmap: real estate services in the app, market insights for builders, AI-assisted dispute resolution, and opening existing homes to investors.
