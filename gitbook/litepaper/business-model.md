# Business model

Sheda Solutions earns when the market works: a small fee on payments that complete, never on money that's returned.

## Fees on completed deals

| Where                    | Fee                                                    | Paid by                                   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Marketplace sale or rent | 2% of the payment                                      | The seller or landlord, from what they receive |
| A raise                  | 2%, only once it fills                                 | From the money raised                     |
| Rent from a flat         | 2%                                                     | From the flat's rent                      |
| Share market             | 2% of each share sale                                  | From the sale                             |

Buyers and renters pay the listed price and nothing more. Browsing and listing are free. Fees are taken by the smart contracts, in the open, so anyone can check them.

## Promoted placements

Owners, landlords and builders can pay to promote a listing or a raise in the app's featured banner, so more people see it.

## Coming next

* **Real estate services.** Property management, legal and maintenance help booked in the app, and insurance from licensed partners.
* **Market insights for builders.** Where homes are wanted, from the market's own record, so builders can raise where demand is.

## Why it works

The fee is small enough to undercut agent commissions by a wide margin, and it's only earned when a deal actually completes. As more deals, raises and rents run through the market, the record of demand grows, which makes the market more useful to every participant.
