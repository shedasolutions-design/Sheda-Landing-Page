# Trust and safety

## Escrow on every deal

When someone chooses Buy now, Rent now or Make an offer, the money goes into escrow: a smart contract on the NEAR blockchain. Neither the seller nor Sheda Solutions can spend it. It moves only by the contract's rules: back to the buyer if the deal doesn't go ahead, or to the seller once the buyer has accepted the agreement and a 24-hour hold has passed.

Escrow only protects money paid in the app. Never pay a seller or agent directly, by bank transfer or in cash, for a property listed on Sheda Solutions.

## Clear rules for walking away

* Before the seller accepts, the buyer can cancel at any time for a full refund. An offer that isn't accepted within 7 days expires, and the buyer reclaims their money.
* After the seller accepts, and until the agreement is sent, the buyer can still cancel for a full refund.
* When the agreement arrives, the buyer can ask for changes, or reject it and cancel for a full refund.
* Accepting the agreement is the buyer's commitment. From then on the deal can't be cancelled; if something is wrong before the payment is released, either side can raise a dispute.

Refunds are always in full. Nothing is taken from money returned.

## Disputes

From the moment an offer is accepted until the payment is released, either side can raise a dispute from the deal. The payment stays in escrow while it's reviewed. Rulings are made by the Sheda Solutions council, an account that needs several of its members to sign, and are carried out by the contract itself.

As the market grows, Sheda Solutions is looking into AI-assisted dispute resolution: reading the deal's record, documents and messages to help settle disputes faster, with people still making the final call.

## Damage deposits

For rentals, the refundable damage deposit stays in escrow for the whole lease. When the lease ends, the landlord has 7 days to claim against it if the property was damaged beyond what both sides agreed. If they don't claim, the whole deposit goes back to the renter, and nobody can stop it. If they do, the claim is reviewed, and whatever isn't awarded to the landlord goes back to the renter.

## Knowing who you deal with

* **Identity checks.** People can verify their identity with an official ID. A verified badge then shows on their profile and listings.
* **Ranks from the record.** Builders and managers are ranked from their record on the blockchain, worked out in the open.
* **Stake.** Builders put their own money behind their raises, as cover for investors.
* **Private by default.** Email and phone numbers are shown only to someone you have an appointment with.

## Account protection

* Signing in on a new device needs a code sent to your email.
* Viewing wallet keys, setting a transaction PIN, changing your email and deleting your account all need your password or an emailed code.
* Sheda Solutions staff will never ask for your codes, password, PIN or recovery phrase.

## Security reviews

No real money moves on Sheda Solutions until an independent security audit of every smart contract is complete. Until then, everything runs on the NEAR test network.
