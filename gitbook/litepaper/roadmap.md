# Roadmap

| # | Stage                            | Status       | What it brings                                                                                     |
| - | -------------------------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | **Marketplace**                  | Early access | Rent, buy and sell directly, with every payment held in escrow.                                    |
| 2 | **Sheda Collections**            | Early access | Own shares in new buildings and earn from their rent.                                              |
| 3 | **Mainnet launch**               | Coming next  | Real payments after an independent security audit, starting in Lagos, with Android and iOS apps.   |
| 4 | **Real estate services**         | Planned      | Property management, legal and maintenance booked in the app, and insurance from licensed partners. |
| 5 | **Market insights for builders** | Planned      | See where homes are wanted before you raise, so new supply goes where demand is.                   |
| 6 | **Bring your own property**      | Planned      | Open a home you already own to investors as shares, and unlock everything it can do.               |

## The order matters

No real money moves on Sheda Solutions until an independent audit of the contracts is done. After mainnet, each step adds a layer to the market: services so owners and managers can get help in the app, insights so builders can see demand before they raise, and bring your own property so owners can open homes they already have to investors.

## Being explored

* **AI-assisted dispute resolution**, so disputes are settled faster as the market grows, with people still making the final call.
* **Student housing raises** near campuses where students can't find safe rooms, as one of the first uses of market insights.

## Today

Sheda Solutions is in early access at [market.sheda.app](https://market.sheda.app). Everything runs on the NEAR test network, so anyone can try every feature without real money.
