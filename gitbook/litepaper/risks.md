# Risks and how they're managed

| Risk                               | How it's managed                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Smart contract faults**          | Every contract is tested, and no real money moves until an independent security audit is complete. Contract changes need several council members to sign.                    |
| **A builder is late or fails**     | Builders are paid phase by phase after inspection; investors can object and stop a build; builders' stake covers investors; ranks show each builder's record.                  |
| **A bad manager**                  | Managers publish their fee and pledge before investors vote; owners of more than half a flat's shares can replace them.                                                        |
| **Fraud between users**            | Money waits in escrow, ownership is on record, identity checks show who people are, and users are warned never to pay outside the app.                                         |
| **Lost wallets**                   | Wallets can be backed up to Google with an encrypted copy, or with a 12-word recovery phrase.                                                                                  |
| **Legal and regulatory change**    | Sheda Solutions works with legal advisers in each market it enters, and launches city by city, starting in Lagos.                                                              |
| **Price swings in crypto**         | Payments are in dollar stablecoins (USDC and USDT), not in volatile coins.                                                                                                     |

## What Sheda Solutions does not promise

Sheda Solutions does not guarantee any return, rent or resale price. Rent shown on a raise is the builder's estimate. Shares may be slow to sell and may sell for less than was paid. Blockchain transactions can't be reversed once confirmed.
