# Technology

People shouldn't need to understand crypto to use Sheda Solutions. You browse, pay and sign like any other app, while the blockchain works in the background.

## Why NEAR

Sheda Solutions is built on the [NEAR](https://near.org) blockchain.

* **Low cost.** Network fees are a tiny fraction of a cent, which makes small payments like monthly rent or a single share practical.
* **Fast.** Transactions confirm in a couple of seconds.
* **Readable accounts.** Wallets have names, not long codes, which makes mistakes less likely.
* **Built to scale.** NEAR splits its work across many parts of the network as use grows.

## What lives on the blockchain

Smart contracts on NEAR hold the money and the records, under rules anyone can check:

* **The marketplace**: property records, offers, escrow, agreements, leases, damage deposits and disputes.
* **Raises**: each Sheda Collections round, its shares, votes and phase payments.
* **Flat vaults**: one per flat, receiving its rent and paying each owner their share.
* **The stake registry**: builders' stake and the cover set aside for each raise.
* **The share market**: owners selling and buying shares between themselves.

Documents themselves are stored off the blockchain, with a fingerprint of each one recorded on it, so anyone can prove a document hasn't been changed.

## A wallet built in

* **Created for you.** A NEAR wallet is made when you sign up, with nothing to install.
* **Kept on your device.** Your wallet lives in your browser or device, not on Sheda's servers. That's what keeps it yours.
* **Backed up in one tap.** Save an encrypted copy to your Google account, locked with a backup password only you know, or write down a 12-word recovery phrase.

## No network fees

Sheda Solutions covers the network fees for actions on its own contracts, so people don't need to hold any NEAR to list, offer, invest or claim rent.

## Dollar coins, local prices

Payments are made in dollar stablecoins (USDC and USDT), so amounts keep their value. Prices are shown in 24 currencies, including naira.

## Open by design

Every listing, payment, inspection and agreement is on the blockchain, open to check and impossible to quietly change. Contract addresses will be published here at mainnet launch, after the security audit.
