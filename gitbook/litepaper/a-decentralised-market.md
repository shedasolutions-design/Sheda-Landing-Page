# A decentralised market

Sheda Solutions is a decentralised market for real estate operations. It doesn't buy, sell, list or finance property itself. The people who use it do, each running their own part of a property's life:

| Role                       | What they do on Sheda Solutions                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Builders and developers** | Raise from many investors to build or buy, where the market shows demand. Put stake behind their profile so investors can trust them. |
| **Investors**              | Own shares in real homes with what they can afford. Vote for the manager, approve each phase, claim their rent and sell when they choose. |
| **Property managers**      | Inspect each building phase, let the finished homes and send the rent to their owners, all on record.                                  |
| **Landlords and sellers**  | List in minutes. Buyers' money is in escrow before they meet, and it's released when the deal is done.                                  |
| **Renters and buyers**     | Rent now, Buy now or Make an offer, then book an appointment to inspect. Their money waits in escrow until they accept.                 |

Sheda Solutions provides the rails: escrow, open records and the tools. The money sits in smart contracts, not with Sheda Solutions.

## The property cycle

The roles connect into one cycle, and each turn of it happens in the open:

1. **Raise.** A builder opens a raise for a building. Many investors each buy shares in a specific flat.
2. **Build or buy.** The builder is paid phase by phase, only after each phase is inspected, while investors can object.
3. **List.** The finished flats are handed to the property manager the investors chose, and listed on the marketplace.
4. **Rent or sell.** Renters and buyers deal through escrow, exactly as with any other listing.
5. **Income.** Rent goes into each flat's own vault on the blockchain, and its owners claim their share directly.

Because every listing, deal and rent payment is on one shared record, the market can show where homes are wanted. Builders can raise where demand is, so money flows to where people actually need homes: student housing near a growing campus, for example, or flats in a fast-growing part of a city.

## What "decentralised" means here

* **Your money isn't held by a company.** Payments wait in smart contracts that follow fixed, public rules.
* **Your records aren't held by a company.** Ownership, deals and agreements are recorded on the NEAR blockchain, where anyone can check them.
* **Your wallet is yours.** It lives on your own device, not on Sheda's servers.
* **Decisions are made by the people involved.** Sellers choose which offer to accept. Investors choose their manager and can stop a build.
