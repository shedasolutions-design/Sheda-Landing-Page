# Investing in Sheda Collections

## How to invest

1. **Choose a project.** Browse raises with photos, plans, the builder's rank and the stake behind them.
2. **Read the project pack.** It holds the builder's documents of title, approvals, costs, timeline and agreements. A raise can't open without one.
3. **Choose a flat and an amount.** Invest as much or as little as you like, above the raise's minimum.
4. **Vote for the manager.** Each manager shows their fee and their pledge: their own terms you can hold them to.
5. **Wait for the round to fill.** Your money waits in the raise contract, not with the builder. You can take it out while the round fills, except in its last 7 days or once it's full. If the round misses its deadline, everyone gets all their money back.

## While it's being built

The builder is paid phase by phase:

1. The builder says a phase is finished.
2. The property manager inspects it in person and signs it off.
3. Owners then have **7 days to object**.
4. If a quarter of the money in the raise objects, the payment is held and the build stops.
5. Otherwise that phase's share of the raise is paid to the builder.

If the builder's own company is the manager, each payment instead needs approval from more than half of the money in the raise.

If the build stops, owners take back what hasn't been paid to the builder yet, plus their share of the stake the builder set aside as cover. Money already paid for finished phases can't be recovered.

## Earning rent

When the building is finished it's handed to the manager, who lets the flats. Each flat's rent goes into its own vault on the blockchain. After the manager's fee, a 5% repair reserve and the 2% fee, the rest is yours in proportion to your shares. Claim it whenever it's ready.

Owners of more than half a flat's shares can replace its manager.

## Selling or buying shares

Sell your shares, or buy more, on the share market. A 2% fee is taken from each sale.

## Risks of investing

Read these before you put money into a raise.

* The builder may be late or fail. Money already paid for finished phases can't be recovered.
* Rent is the builder's estimate, not a promise. Months without a tenant earn nothing.
* The manager matters: read their pledge and fee.
* Your shares may be slow to sell, and may sell for less than you paid.
* Blockchain transactions can't be reversed once confirmed.
* If you lose access to your wallet, you lose access to your shares and rent. Back it up.
* Smart contracts are tested, but no software is free of risk.
* Sheda Solutions does not guarantee any return, rent or resale price.
