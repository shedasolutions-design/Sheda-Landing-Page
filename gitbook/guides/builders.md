# For builders

## 1. Register as a builder

Register your company in the app and add your licences and registration documents to your profile. Investors see them before they invest; if none are shown, they're told to ask you for them.

## 2. Put stake behind your profile

A stake is your own money, held in the stake registry on the blockchain. It isn't a fee and it isn't paid to Sheda Solutions. It stays yours, and it stands behind the promises you make to investors.

* **It protects investors.** If a build stops, they take back the money not yet paid to you, plus their share of the cover set aside from your stake.
* **It earns trust.** Investors see how much cover each raise carries, and your stake and anything ever taken from it are on your public profile.
* **Free stake** can back a new raise or be withdrawn at any time. **Stake set aside as cover** for a live raise is locked until that raise ends.

## 3. Prepare your project pack

Every raise needs a project pack: documents of title, approvals, costs, timeline and all agreements. Its fingerprint is recorded on the blockchain when the raise opens, so it can't be swapped later.

## 4. Suggest managers

Suggest one to three property managers near the project. Each one accepts with their fee and a pledge. Investors vote for the one they want. If no manager is available, your own company can manage, and investors are clearly told.

## 5. Open the raise

Set the flats, the target, the minimum investment, the deadline and the building phases. When the round fills, each flat's vault is opened automatically, the chosen manager is fixed and building begins. If it doesn't fill by the deadline, every investor is refunded in full.

## 6. Get paid phase by phase

When a phase is finished, the manager inspects and signs it off. Investors have 7 days to object, then that phase's share is paid to you, less the 2% fee on the raise.

## Your rank

Your rank comes from your record on the blockchain: jobs finished on time, late or abandoned, and your stake. It needs at least 2 finished projects; until then your profile reads "Not rated yet".
