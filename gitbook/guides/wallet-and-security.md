# Your wallet and security

## Your wallet

Your Sheda wallet is created for you and kept in your browser or device, not on Sheda's servers. That's what keeps it yours. It also means you need a backup.

## Two ways to back up

* **Google backup**: an encrypted copy saved to your Google account, locked with a backup password only you know.
* **Recovery phrase**: 12 words that restore the wallet anywhere. Write them down and keep them offline.

Your backup password can't be reset. Nobody, including Sheda Solutions, can unlock your backup without it. If you forget it while your wallet is still on a device, set a new one under **Backup & recovery**.

## Signing in on a new device

Sign in, enter the code sent to your email, then restore your wallet from your Google backup or recovery phrase. Don't create a new wallet: your deals and money stay with the one you have. The device is then remembered for 30 days.

## Keeping your account safe

* Viewing your wallet keys, setting a transaction PIN, changing your email and deleting your account all need your password or an emailed code.
* Sheda Solutions staff will never ask for your codes, password, PIN or recovery phrase.
* If an email says your Sheda email was changed and it wasn't you, reply to it straight away.
