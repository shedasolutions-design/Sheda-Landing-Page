# Fees

## On the marketplace

2% of each completed sale or rental payment, taken from the amount paid to the seller or landlord. Buyers and renters pay the listed price and nothing more. Browsing and listing are free, and there's no agent commission.

## On Sheda Collections

* 2% of a raise, only once it fills.
* From rent: the manager's fee (shown on their profile), 5% kept in a repair reserve for the flat, and the 2% fee.
* 2% of share sales between owners.

## Never on refunds

Money returned to you, from a cancelled offer, a rejected agreement or a raise that doesn't fill, is returned in full.

## No network fees

Sheda Solutions covers the blockchain's network fees for actions on its contracts, so you don't need to hold any NEAR.

## Promoted placements

Paying to feature a listing or a raise in the promoted banner is optional.
