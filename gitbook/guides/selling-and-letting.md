# Selling and letting

## The steps

1. **List the property** with photos, price and details. It's recorded on the blockchain as yours. You can edit the listing at any time.
2. **Meet serious buyers.** Buyers and renters pay into escrow with Buy now, Rent now or an offer. You see the money is there before you do anything.
3. **Accept the offer you want.** The others are returned to their buyers automatically.
4. **Approve appointments.** The buyer asks for a time to inspect, within the days and hours you set. Approve or decline each request; when you decline you can add a note and suggest another time.
5. **Send the agreement** and documents from the deal. Each document can have several pages or images, or be a PDF.
6. **Get paid.** When the buyer accepts, your payment arrives after a 24-hour hold, less the 2% fee.

## Set your appointment days and hours

Under **Appointment Hours**, tick the days you take appointments and set one time range. Any day can have its own hours: tap **+** to add another. Times are in 30-minute steps. Buyers ask for times inside your hours; other days are greyed out.

Several buyers can ask for the same time. Nothing is booked until you approve it. If you set no hours, buyers can ask for any future time.

## Asking for a refundable damage deposit

* When you let a property you can ask for a refundable damage deposit. The tenant pays it with the rent.
* It's held in escrow for the whole lease. It isn't paid to you.
* If the property is damaged beyond what you both agreed in the rent agreement, claim against it within 7 days of the lease ending. The claim is reviewed.
* If you don't claim, it goes back to the tenant.

## Personal or business

If you have an agency or business profile, you can reply to buyers as yourself or as your business. Chats are kept one per property.

## Promote a listing

You can pay to feature a listing in the app's promoted banner so more people see it.
