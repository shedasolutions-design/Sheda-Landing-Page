# Getting started

Sheda Solutions runs in your browser, on any phone or computer, with nothing to download. Android and iOS apps are coming with the mainnet launch.

## 1. Open the app

Go to [market.sheda.app](https://market.sheda.app).

## 2. Create your account

Sign up with your email, or continue with Google. A NEAR wallet is created for you automatically. You don't need to know anything about crypto.

## 3. Back up your wallet

Your wallet lives on your device, not on Sheda's servers, so back it up straight away:

* **Google backup**: an encrypted copy saved to your Google account, locked with a backup password only you know.
* **Recovery phrase**: 12 words that restore the wallet anywhere. Write them down and keep them offline.

See [Your wallet and security](wallet-and-security.md) for more.
