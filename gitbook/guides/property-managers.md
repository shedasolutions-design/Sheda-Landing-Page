# For property managers

Property managers run buildings for their owners on Sheda Collections. They are chosen by the investors, by vote.

## What you do

* **Accept to be suggested.** A builder suggests you for a raise. Accept with your fee and your pledge: your own terms investors can hold you to.
* **Inspect each phase.** When the builder says a phase is finished, inspect it in person and sign it off. Investors then have 7 days to object before the builder is paid.
* **Take the handover.** When the building is finished, it's handed to you.
* **Let the flats.** List them on the marketplace like any other property. Renters pay through escrow.
* **Send the rent.** Each flat's rent goes into its vault, where your fee, a 5% repair reserve and the 2% fee are taken, and the rest goes to its owners.

## Your record

Your profile shows your record: jobs finished, on time or late, and your rank. Owners of more than half a flat's shares can replace you, so your pledge matters.
