# Business model, projections and risk

## Revenue streams

Sheda Solutions earns when the market works: a small fee on payments that complete, taken by the smart contracts in the open, and never on money that's returned.

| Stream                          | Rate     | Charged on                                                                       |
| ------------------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Marketplace transactions**    | 2%       | Each completed sale or rental payment, from the amount paid to the seller or landlord |
| **Raises**                      | 2%       | The money raised, only once a round fills                                        |
| **Rent from Collections flats** | 2%       | Each flat's rent, alongside the manager's fee and the 5% repair reserve          |
| **Share market**                | 2%       | Each share sale between owners                                                   |
| **Promoted placements**         | Per slot | Featuring a listing or raise in the app's promoted banner                        |
| **Services (planned)**          | Per job  | Management, legal and maintenance booked through the app                         |
| **Market insights (planned)**   | Per plan | Demand data for builders and developers                                          |

Buyers and renters pay the listed price and nothing more. Browsing and listing are free, and there are no network fees.

## Why the model scales

* **Aligned incentives.** Revenue only comes from completed deals, so the market earns when its users succeed.
* **Undercuts agents.** 2% is far below typical agent commissions stacked on top of rent or price.
* **Compounding activity.** One raise produces a fee on the raise, on every rent payment for the life of the building, and on every share trade.
* **Data network effect.** Every deal and rent payment adds to the record of demand, which powers market insights and draws more builders.

## Risk register

| Risk                              | Mitigation                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Smart contract vulnerability**  | Unit tests and end-to-end runs on the test network; independent security audit before mainnet; state-before-transfer pattern; multi-signature council. |
| **Builder delay or failure**      | Phase payments after inspection; investor objections; ring-fenced builder stake; public ranks from on-chain history.                                   |
| **Poor property management**      | Published fee and pledge before the vote; capped vault fees; replacement by more than half a flat's shares; recorded reserve draws.                    |
| **Fraud between users**           | Escrow on every payment; on-chain ownership; identity checks; warnings never to pay outside the app.                                                  |
| **Lost wallets**                  | Encrypted Google backup or 12-word recovery phrase; emailed codes for new devices.                                                                    |
| **Stablecoin and currency risk**  | Settlement in dollar stablecoins; local currencies used for display only.                                                                              |
| **Legal and regulatory change**   | Legal advisers in each market; city-by-city launch starting in Lagos; regulated services (such as insurance) only through licensed partners.          |
| **Market competition**            | Escrow, open records, fractional ownership with control, and a single market for every role, all in one app.                                          |

Sheda Solutions does not guarantee any return, rent or resale price. Rent shown on a raise is the builder's estimate, shares may be slow to sell, and blockchain transactions can't be reversed once confirmed.
