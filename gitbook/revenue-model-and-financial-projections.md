# Revenue model and financial projections

Income Streams:

* Transaction Fees: 2% on each completed transaction, whether buying, selling or renting, taken from the amount paid to the seller or landlord. Buyers and tenants pay the listed price and nothing more. Nothing is taken from refunds.
* Sheda Collections Fees: 2% of a raise once it fills, 2% of each flat's rent, and 2% of share sales between owners.
* Promoted Placements: Owners, landlords and builders can pay to feature a listing or a raise in the app's promoted banner.
* Premium Features and Services: Additional income from services like professional management tools, real estate services booked in the app, and market insights for builders.

**Risk management**

* Legal and Regulatory Risks: We will engage with legal experts to navigate varying international real estate laws, launching city by city, starting in Lagos.
* Cybersecurity: An independent security audit of every smart contract before real money moves, and ongoing audits and updates to secure the platform against vulnerabilities.
* Investment Risks: Builders are paid phase by phase after inspection, investors can object, and builders' stake stands behind each raise. Sheda Solutions does not guarantee any return, rent or resale price.
* Market Competition: Differentiation through niche focus on secured and ease of access housing and robust P2P features.
