# Roadmap

One open market for everything real estate, built one layer at a time. No real money moves until an independent security audit of the contracts is complete.

## Phase 1: Core marketplace <mark style="color:green;">· Live in early access</mark>

* Listings minted on the blockchain; search by location, budget and type.
* Buy now, Rent now and Make an offer, with every payment in escrow.
* Appointment requests with seller-set days and hours.
* Agreements and multi-page documents per deal; redraft or cancel.
* Built-in wallet with Google and 12-word backup; sponsored network fees.
* Chat per property, deal tracking and notifications; prices in 24 currencies.

## Phase 2: Smart contract escrow and dispute resolution <mark style="color:green;">· Live in early access</mark>

* 24-hour time lock on release; 7-day offer expiry; automatic refunds.
* Refundable damage deposits with a 7-day landlord claim window.
* Disputes frozen in escrow and ruled on by the multi-signature council.

## Phase 3: Fractional ownership (Sheda Collections) <mark style="color:green;">· Live in early access</mark>

* Raises per flat with project packs, manager nominations and money-weighted votes.
* Phase-by-phase payments with inspection and a 7-day objection window.
* Builder stake registry with ring-fenced cover; public ranks.
* Flat vaults opened automatically at close; on-chain rent split and claims.
* Share market for owners.

## Phase 4: Mainnet launch <mark style="color:orange;">· Coming next</mark>

* Independent security audit of every contract.
* Mainnet deployment with real USDC and USDT, starting in **Lagos**.
* Android and iOS apps.

## Phase 5: Real estate services and AI <mark style="color:blue;">· Planned</mark>

* Property management, legal and maintenance help booked in the app, and insurance from licensed partners.
* AI-assisted dispute resolution: mediation and case preparation, with people making the final call.
* AI matching of homes to renters' and buyers' preferences.

## Phase 6: Market insights and expansion <mark style="color:blue;">· Planned</mark>

* **Market insights for builders**: where homes are wanted, from the market's own record, starting with student housing near campuses.
* **Bring your own property**: owners open homes they already have to investors as shares.
* Chain abstraction, so users can pay from other blockchains.
* Expansion city by city across Nigeria and Africa.
