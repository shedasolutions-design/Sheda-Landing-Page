# Our Roadmap

operational phases

Phase 1: Core Platform Development <mark style="color:green;">(live in early access)</mark>

* Launch of the marketplace with property listing, escrow-protected transactions, appointment requests and a built-in wallet, focused on providing homes where the demand is, from student housing to urban markets.

Phase 2: Smart Contract and Dispute Resolution <mark style="color:green;">(live in early access)</mark>

* Smart contracts for escrow, agreements, refundable damage deposits and a transparent dispute resolution system ruled by the council. Integration of AI agents to resolve disputes more efficiently as we grow.

Phase 3: Fractional ownerships Framework <mark style="color:green;">(live in early access as Sheda Collections)</mark>

* Community-funded real estate projects with governance and decision making based on investment weight: investors vote for the manager, approve each building phase, claim their rent and trade shares on the share market.

Phase 4: Expansion and Refinement <mark style="color:orange;">(coming next)</mark>

* Mainnet launch with real payments after an independent security audit, starting in Lagos, with Android and iOS apps.
* Real estate services: property management, legal and maintenance booked in the app, and insurance from licensed partners.
* Market insights for builders: see where homes are wanted before they raise, so new supply goes where the demand is.
* Bring your own property: open a home you already own to investors as shares.
* Scaling to more cities and ongoing platform enhancements.
