The problem

Property still runs on blind trust

Renting, buying or investing in property has always meant handing money to strangers and middlemen and hoping for the best.

Agent fees on top of the price

Renters and buyers pay one or more agents on top of the rent or price, often for little more than an introduction.

Fake listings, homes sold twice

It's hard to check who really owns a property, so the same home can be "sold" to more than one person.

Deposits that disappear

Money paid upfront for an inspection, a reservation or a damage deposit is often never seen again.

Paperwork that gets lost

Agreements live on paper, in someone's drawer, and are hard to prove later.

Investing is closed to most people

Property needs a large sum at once. Small investors who are offered a share get no say and no view of the money.

Supply goes to the wrong places

Builders guess where homes are wanted, while the people who need them have no way to signal demand.

Student housing shows it clearly. Each year hundreds of thousands of students move to study, but only a small share find a place on campus. The rest depend on an off-campus market full of scams, inflated rents and unsafe rooms, while new homes often aren't built near the campuses that need them.

Nigeria alone has a housing shortage estimated at around 15 million homes. The money and the demand exist. What's missing is a way for people to deal with each other directly, safely and in the open, and for new homes to be built where they're actually wanted.

The idea

A decentralised market for real estate operations

Sheda Solutions doesn't buy, sell, list or finance property itself. Every role in real estate acts independently, on one shared record. Sheda Solutions provides the rails: escrow, open records and the tools.

Builders

Raise from many investors to build or buy, where the market shows demand. Put stake behind their profile so investors can trust them.

Investors

Own shares in real homes with what they can afford. Vote for the manager, approve each phase, claim their rent and sell when they choose.

Property managers

Inspect each building phase, let the finished homes and send the rent to their owners, all on record.

Landlords and sellers

List in minutes. Buyers' money is in escrow before they meet, and it's released when the deal is done.

Renters and buyers

Rent now, Buy now or Make an offer, then book an appointment to inspect. Their money waits in escrow until they accept.

Everyone

Every listing, payment, inspection and agreement is on the NEAR blockchain, open to check and impossible to quietly change.

The property cycle

Each turn happens in the open, so money flows to where people actually need homes.

1 · RaiseMany investors buy shares in a flat.
2 · BuildPaid phase by phase, after inspection.
3 · ListThe chosen manager lists the flats.
4 · Rent or sellRenters deal through escrow.
5 · IncomeRent goes to each flat's owners.

Because every listing, deal and rent payment is on one shared record, the market shows where homes are wanted, from student housing near a growing campus to flats in a fast-growing part of a city. Builders can raise where demand is.

The marketplace

Rent, buy and sell directly, with every payment in escrow

Every deal follows the same order, so both sides always know what comes next and where the money is.

Secure it. Choose Buy now or Rent now at the listed price, or Make an offer. The money goes into escrow, not to the seller.

The seller decides. They accept the offer they want; every other offer's money goes straight back.

Book appointment. Ask for a time to inspect within the seller's days and hours. The seller approves it, or suggests another time.

Agreement. The seller sends the agreement and documents. Accept, ask for changes, or reject and cancel for a full refund.

Payment. Once the buyer accepts, the payment is released to the seller after a 24-hour hold.

  • Ownership on record, so a home can't quietly be sold twice.
  • Appointments on request: nothing is booked until the seller approves it.
  • Refundable damage deposits held in escrow for the whole lease, not by the landlord.
  • Documents always to hand, with several pages or a PDF each.
  • One chat per property; reply as yourself or as your business.
  • Prices in 24 currencies, including naira.

Sheda Collections · fractional ownership

Own a share of a home. Have a say. Earn its rent.

Builders raise from many investors to build or buy. Each investor owns shares in one specific flat, and the rent it earns is shared between its owners in proportion to their shares.

How a raise runs

The builder opens a raise with a project pack: title, approvals, costs, timeline and agreements, fingerprinted on the blockchain.

Investors choose a flat and an amount, and vote for the property manager. Their money waits in the raise contract.

While it fills, investors can withdraw, except in the last 7 days or once it's full. If it misses its deadline, everyone is refunded in full.

When it fills, the chosen manager is fixed and building begins, paid phase by phase.

Shares, votes and rent in one place

Choose the manager

Owners vote for the manager. More than half a flat's shares can replace them.

Approve every phase

The manager inspects each phase; owners have 7 days to object. A quarter of the money objecting stops the build.

See every payment

Each flat's rent lands in its own vault on the blockchain, visible to every owner.

Claim income directly

After the manager's fee, a 5% repair reserve and the 2% fee, owners claim their rent themselves.

Exit on their terms

Sell shares, or buy more, on the open share market.

Builders have stake

Their own money stands behind each raise as cover for investors if a build stops.

Any return shown on a raise is the builder's own estimate, not a promise. Builders and managers are ranked from their record on the blockchain, worked out in the open.

Trust and technology

Clear rules, open records, nothing to install

Escrow on every deal

Payments wait in a smart contract. Neither the seller nor Sheda Solutions can spend them; they move only by the contract's public rules.

Clear rules for walking away

Full refunds before the agreement is accepted. Offers expire after 7 days. Refunds are always in full.

Disputes

Either side can raise one until payment is released; the money stays in escrow. Rulings need several council members to sign. AI-assisted resolution is being explored to settle disputes faster.

Damage deposits

Held in escrow for the lease. The landlord has 7 days after it ends to claim; otherwise it all goes back to the renter.

Know who you deal with

Identity checks with official ID, ranks from the record, and contact details shared only after an appointment.

Protected accounts

Emailed codes for new devices and every important change. Staff never ask for codes or recovery phrases.

Built on NEAR

Low cost, fast and readable. The blockchain works quietly in the background.

Wallet built in

Created at sign-up, kept on your own device, backed up to Google in one tap or with a 12-word phrase.

No network fees

Sheda Solutions covers network fees on its contracts. No one needs to hold crypto to use it.

Dollar coins

Payments in USDC and USDT, so amounts keep their value. Prices shown in 24 currencies.

Documents are stored off the blockchain with a fingerprint of each recorded on it, so anyone can prove a document hasn't changed. No real money moves until an independent security audit of every contract is complete.

Business model

A small fee, only when a deal completes

Browsing and listing are free, with no agent fees and no network fees. Fees are taken by the smart contracts, in the open, and never from money that's returned.

WhereFeePaid from
Marketplace sale or rent2%What the seller or landlord receives. Buyers pay the listed price.
A raise2%The money raised, only once the round fills
Rent from a flat2%The flat's rent, alongside the manager's fee and a 5% repair reserve
Share market2%Each share sale between owners
Promoted placementsOptionalOwners and builders pay to feature a listing or raise in the app's banner
RefundsNoneCancelled offers, rejected agreements and unfilled raises are returned in full

Why it works

2% undercuts agent commissions by a wide margin and is only earned when a deal actually completes. Every deal, raise and rent adds to the record of demand, which makes the market more useful to everyone in it.

Risks and how they're managed

Smart contract faultsTested contracts, an independent audit before real money, and council sign-off for changes.
A builder is late or failsPhase-by-phase payment after inspection, investor objections, builders' stake and public ranks.
A bad managerPublished fee and pledge before the vote; replaceable by more than half a flat's shares.
Fraud between usersEscrow, ownership on record, identity checks, and never paying outside the app.
Legal and regulatory changeLegal advisers in each market, launching city by city, starting in Lagos.

Sheda Solutions does not guarantee any return, rent or resale price. Shares may be slow to sell. Blockchain transactions can't be reversed once confirmed.
