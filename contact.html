FAQs

Quick answers

How do I get started?

Open market.sheda.app in your browser and create a free account. You can start browsing homes or list your property straight away. There's nothing to download.

What if something goes wrong with a deal?

Your money stays in escrow until both sides have done their part, so it can't simply vanish. Every step is recorded, and if you need help, our support team is one message away.

Do I have to use cryptocurrency?

You see every price in your own currency, including naira, and pay through the wallet built into the app. Payments settle in USDC, a stable digital dollar, so the amount you agree is the amount that moves.

How does blockchain keep my deals safe?

Every listing, payment and ownership record is stored on the blockchain, where it's permanent and open to check. No one can quietly change it, not even Sheda.
