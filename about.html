About Sheda

Your real estate operations, simplified

Renting, buying, selling and investing in property, all in one place you can trust.

Our mission is to make real estate affable, available, affordable and secure for everyone, everywhere.

Our story

Revolutionizing real estate operations

Real estate has always run on trust, and trust has always been expensive. Buyers pay agents on top of the price. Tenants hand over deposits and hope. Owners chase payments and paperwork. And everyone wonders whether the listing in front of them is even real.

Sheda was built to change that. We bring buyers, renters, owners and investors together directly, and let technology hold the money and the records instead of a middleman. The result: property that's faster, cheaper and safer to deal in, for everyone.

Why blockchain

Records no one can quietly change

On the blockchain, every listing, payment and ownership record is permanent and open to check. Nobody can edit it behind your back: not a buyer, not a seller, not even us.

Payments run through smart contracts, simple programs that follow the agreed rules exactly, every time. You don't need to understand any of it. You just get a deal you can rely on.

The platform

Everything property, in one app

From your first search to the day you get the keys, and long after.

Browse real listings, compare properties and talk straight to the owner. Every listing is recorded on the blockchain, so you always know who you're dealing with.

List, let and sell from one place. Set your viewing hours, track offers and get paid through escrow, with property management services coming soon.

Less paperwork, more certainty

Agreements are signed and stored securely, so your documents are ready on any device, any time. No lost files, no forged copies.

Deal directly. Pay safely.

No intermediaries slowing things down. Agree the terms with the other side, and your money is released from escrow only once you're satisfied.

  • Faster negotiations
  • You decide when the money moves
