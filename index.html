FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to us

How does escrow protect my money?

When you pay, the money doesn't go straight to the other side. It's held by a smart contract, a program no one can tamper with, and released only when the deal is complete. Not a buyer, a seller or even Sheda can take it early.

Is my property ownership secure?

Yes. Ownership is recorded on the NEAR blockchain, so nobody can quietly change it. You can check your proof of ownership yourself, any time, without asking anyone.

Do I need to understand crypto or blockchain?

Not at all. The wallet is built into the app, so there's nothing to install, and you can back it up to your Google account in one tap. You browse, pay and sign just like any other app, while the blockchain works quietly in the background.

How does investing in Sheda Collections work?

Choose a new building, buy as many shares as you like, and receive your share of the rent once it's finished and let. Builders are paid in stages, and only after each stage passes inspection, so your money moves only as the building rises.

How do I earn from my own property?

Rent it out on a lease Sheda enforces for you, or sell it with payment released on the agreed terms. You can also open it to investors as shares, with the rent shared out automatically.

Can I use Sheda today?

Yes. Sheda is in early access and runs in your browser, with nothing to download. During early access, transactions run on the NEAR test network, so you can explore every feature risk-free while we complete our independent security audit.
