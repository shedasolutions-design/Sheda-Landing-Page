FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to us

Who runs the deals on Sheda Solutions?

You do. Sheda Solutions is a decentralised market: builders, investors, property managers, landlords, buyers and renters each run their own part of a property's life. Sheda Solutions provides the rails, the escrow, the open records and the tools, and the money sits in smart contracts, not with Sheda Solutions or anyone else.

How does escrow protect my money?

When you pay, the money doesn't go straight to the other side. It's held by a smart contract, a program no one can tamper with, and released only when the deal is complete. Not a buyer, a seller or even Sheda can take it early.

Is my property ownership secure?

Yes. Ownership is recorded on the NEAR blockchain, so nobody can quietly change it. You can check your proof of ownership yourself, any time, without asking anyone.

Do I need to understand crypto or blockchain?

Not at all. The wallet is built into the app, so there's nothing to install, and you can back it up to your Google account in one tap. You browse, pay and sign just like any other app, while the blockchain works quietly in the background.

How does investing in Sheda Collections work?

Choose a building that's raising, buy as many shares as you like, and vote for the property manager who will run it. Builders are paid in stages, only after each stage passes inspection, so your money moves only as the building rises. Once it's finished and let, you claim your share of the rent, and you can sell your shares on the share market whenever you choose.

How do I earn from my own property?

Rent it out with the rent and a refundable damage deposit held in escrow, or sell it with payment released on the agreed terms. Opening a home you already own to investors as shares is on our roadmap.

What does it cost?

Browsing and listing are free, with no agent fees and no network fees. When a payment completes, a 2% network fee is taken by the smart contract, in the open. A cancelled or refunded deal costs nothing.

Can I use Sheda today?

Yes. Sheda is in early access and runs in your browser, with nothing to download. During early access, transactions run on the NEAR test network, so you can explore every feature risk-free while we complete our independent security audit.
