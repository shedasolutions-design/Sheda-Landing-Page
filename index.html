FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is my property ownership secure?

Yes. Ownership is recorded on the NEAR blockchain, so nobody can quietly change it: not a buyer, not a seller, not even Sheda. You can check your proof of ownership yourself, any time.

How do I earn from my property?

Rent it out on a lease Sheda enforces for you, or sell it with payment released on the agreed terms. You can also open it to investors as shares, with the rent shared out automatically.

What else can I do once my property is on Sheda?

Rent it, manage it, or open it to investors. As property management, legal and insurance services arrive, they'll work with it from day one.

Do I need to understand blockchain?

Not at all. The wallet is built into the app, so there's nothing to install. You browse, pay and sign like in any other app, and the blockchain works quietly in the background.

Can I use Sheda today?

Yes. Sheda is in early access and runs in your browser, with no download needed. During early access, transactions run on the NEAR test network, so you can explore every feature risk-free while we complete our independent security audit.

Ready to find your next property?

Sheda runs right in your browser. No download, no agent fees.
