FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is my property ownership secure on the blockchain?

Ownership is recorded on NEAR by a smart contract, so no party can quietly change it. Not a buyer, not a seller, not us. Your NFT receipt is proof you can check yourself, at any time, without asking anyone.

How do I earn from a property once it is listed?

Let it to verified tenants on a lease the contract enforces, or sell it with payment released on the agreed terms. You can also split it into shares and let investors hold part of it, with the returns divided between them automatically.

What can I do with a property once it is on chain?

Let it, manage it, or open it to investors as shares. It also becomes a verified asset the rest of Sheda can work with as management, legal and insurance services arrive.

Do I need to understand blockchain to use this?

No. The wallet is built into the app, so there is no extension to install and no seed phrase to look after. You browse, pay and sign the way you would in any other app, and the chain stays behind it.

Is this live, and is it holding real money?

The app runs in your browser today. The marketplace is live, and fractional ownership runs on NEAR testnet with test funds. Nothing has been audited yet, so nothing here is asking you to risk anything.
